How it works

Dragon chickens are not random. They are tied to the path of wandering overlord zombies.

  1. Find a wandering zombie and watch where it starts and stops.
  2. Wait for it to fully stop and choose its next route.
  3. Follow the new path it takes.
  4. Hit the first chicken on that path.

If it is not working, wait until the next chicken spawn reset or move to a fresh area. Someone may have already farmed that set.
